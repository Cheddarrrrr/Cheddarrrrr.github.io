Questions

Good to know.

After you book, we'll text you to confirm a pickup window. We come to your door, collect your console, and give you a written estimate before any work starts. Once it's repaired, we deliver it back to you — usually within 24 hours.

Most common repairs are done within a single day. More involved fixes, or ones that need a specific part ordered, may take a little longer — we'll always give you a clear timeline at pickup.

Then you don't pay. Our diagnostics are free, and if a repair isn't possible or doesn't hold, there's no charge. No fix, no fee — simple as that.

For the vast majority of repairs, your storage is never touched and your saves stay exactly where they are. If a repair could possibly affect your data, we'll tell you upfront before doing anything.

You pay on delivery, once your console is back and working. We accept cash, Zelle, Venmo, and most cards — whatever's easiest for you.

PlayStation 4 and 5, Xbox One and Series X/S, and every Nintendo Switch model — original, Lite, and OLED. Not sure if yours qualifies? Just ask in the booking form.
