2. What we do

We repair current-generation consoles and gaming handhelds, including:

  • PlayStation 5
  • Xbox Series X and Series S
  • Nintendo Switch 2 and Nintendo Switch OLED
  • Steam Deck
  • ASUS ROG Ally
  • Controllers for the above systems

Common jobs include HDMI port replacement, disc drive repair, USB-C and charging port repair, overheating and fan service with thermal compound replacement, controller stick drift repair, and board-level and liquid damage work. We do not sell consoles, games, accessories, or subscriptions — we only sell repair labour and the parts used in those repairs.

We serve Miami Beach, Coral Gables, Coconut Grove, Brickell, Key Biscayne and Pinecrest, and the wider Miami-Dade County area on request. Pickup and delivery are included in every quoted price. There is no travel fee, trip charge, or delivery surcharge.

3. Booking a pickup

You book by submitting the form at repairstationmiami.com, or by calling or texting us. A submitted booking is a request, not a confirmed appointment. We'll contact you — normally within a few hours, and no later than 12 hours — to confirm a pickup window.

A booking becomes an agreement only once we've confirmed the window with you and collected the device.

4. Diagnostics & estimates

Diagnostics are free, on every device, with no obligation. If you don't like the estimate, we return your device at no charge.

After we collect your device we diagnose the fault and give you a written estimate — by text or email — before any work begins. The estimate lists the fault we found, the work proposed, the parts required, and the total price.

No work starts until you approve that estimate. If we open the device and find a second, unrelated fault, or the repair turns out to be more involved than the estimate assumed, we stop and send you a revised estimate. You are never charged more than the figure you approved without approving a new one first.

6. Cancellations & refunds

Cancelling before work starts

You can cancel at any time before you approve the written estimate, for any reason, at no cost. If we've already collected the device, we return it free of charge. There is no cancellation fee and no diagnostic fee.

No fix, no fee

If we can't complete the repair, or the repair doesn't hold, you are not charged for our labour. If you've already paid, we refund that amount to your original payment method.

Parts already installed

One exception. Some repairs need parts we order specifically for your device — this is common on Steam Deck, ROG Ally, and board-level work. Once such a part has been ordered and installed, its cost is non-refundable, even if a separate, later fault means the device can't be fully restored. The labour is still free under "no fix, no fee".

We will always tell you, in the written estimate, when a repair involves a special-order part and what that part costs, before you approve it. If you'd rather not take that risk, decline the estimate and we'll return the device free.

Refund timing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Card refunds processed through Stripe typically appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank. Cash, Zelle, Cash App and Venmo payments are refunded by the same method, normally within 2 business days.

How to request a refund

Call or text (305) 699-2750 or email contact@repairstationmiami.com. We respond to every refund request within 2 business days. Please contact us before opening a dispute with your bank — almost everything is faster to fix directly.

7. 30-day warranty

Every completed repair carries a 30-day warranty from the date we return the device. If the same fault comes back within 30 days, we collect the device, re-repair it, and return it at no charge.

Our first remedy is always to re-repair. If we can't resolve the same fault on a second attempt, we refund what you paid for that repair, less any non-refundable special-order parts described in section 6.

The warranty covers the specific repair we performed and the parts we installed. It does not cover:

  • A new or unrelated fault that appears after the repair
  • New physical damage, drops, or liquid exposure after we returned the device
  • Damage from someone else opening or working on the device after us
  • Normal wear, or faults caused by software, firmware, or system updates

8. Turnaround times

Most repairs are completed and delivered back within 3 days of pickup. That's a typical timeframe, not a guarantee — board-level work, liquid damage, and repairs needing a special-order part can take longer.

If your repair is going to run past 3 days, we tell you and give you a revised estimate of when it'll be ready. You can cancel and have the device returned at any point while it's still with us, subject to section 6.

9. Your data & saved games

For the vast majority of repairs we don't touch your storage, and your saved games, accounts and downloads are untouched. Some repairs — particularly storage, board-level, and liquid damage work — carry a genuine risk of data loss. Where that risk exists, we tell you in the written estimate, before you approve it.

We do not have the means to back up console storage for you. Please back up anything you can't lose before we collect the device. We are not responsible for lost saves, screenshots, or downloaded content.

We don't browse your files, accounts, or media. We only interact with your device as far as diagnosing and fixing the reported fault requires. How we handle your personal information is covered in our Privacy Policy.

  • You'll be reachable during your confirmed pickup and delivery windows, and that someone aged 18 or over will hand over and receive the device.

11. Unclaimed devices

If your repair is finished and we can't reach you or complete delivery, we'll hold the device and keep trying to contact you by phone, text and email. After 60 days from our first delivery attempt, with no response from you, we may treat the device as abandoned and dispose of it in accordance with Florida law. We'd much rather return it — please keep your contact details current.

In any of these cases you're charged nothing and we return the device.
