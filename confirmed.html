Thanks — your pickup request is in. Here's exactly what happens from here, step by step.

01

We get in touch

Within 12 hours we'll reach out on your preferred contact method to confirm a pickup window and ask any follow-up questions about the fault. You'll get a written quote — nothing gets picked up, and no work begins, until you've approved it.

02

We collect your console

On your preferred pickup day we come to your door and take it away. Pickup and delivery are already included in your quote — there's nothing extra to pay for the trip.

03

We show you it working

Once the repair is finished we send you a video of your console up and running. That's when you choose how to pay: securely online, or in person on the day.

04

We schedule your drop-off

Last step — we agree a delivery date and time that suits you, and hand your console back tested, working, and covered by a 30-day warranty.

NO FIX, NO FEE If we can't fix it, you don't pay a cent — and every completed repair carries a 30-day warranty on parts and labor.
